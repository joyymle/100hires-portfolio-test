# Kevin Indig: Enterprise SEO & AI Overviews Strategy

## Summary
Kevin Indig (Former Director of SEO at Shopify & Growth Advisor) focuses heavily on the macroeconomic shifts in search. In 2026, his framework addresses the reality of **Zero-Click Searches** driven by Google's AI Overviews (SGE) and large language models. For enterprise and large-scale web properties, the goal has shifted from acquiring raw keyword traffic to stabilizing the brand's **"Market Share of Voice"** across conversational AI nodes.

---

## Core Strategy Shifts (Traditional Enterprise vs. 2026 AI Search)

### 1. Shift from Traffic Volume to Attribution Volume
* **Old Model:** Track organic traffic numbers and impressions in Google Search Console. Higher volume equaled success.
* **2026 Model:** Track **AI Reference Share**. Since AI Overviews synthesize answers directly on the SERP, websites must optimize to be featured as the definitive source citation inside the AI answer box, prioritizing conversions over vanity clicks.

### 2. Conversational Moats vs. Traditional Backlinks
* Backlinks still hold value for domain authority, but AI crawlers prioritize websites that provide structured, verifiable facts. Enterprise properties must build proprietary data layers and clear entity connections so LLMs can easily reference them without indexing delays.

---

## The Enterprise AI Overviews Optimization Workflow

| Phase | Strategy | Corporate Execution SOP |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **Query Blueprinting** | Identify core high-value queries currently triggering AI Overviews. Categorize them into informational, transactional, and comparative intents. |
| **02** | **Entity Alignment** | Format data utilizing structured Schema markup (Organization, Product, Article). Ensure the brand name is consistently linked with its primary industry entity across the web. |
| **03** | **BLUF Deployment** | Implement the **Bottom Line Up Front (BLUF)** framework on all enterprise landing pages. Provide a clear, direct 2-3 sentence answer at the absolute top of the page for AI scrapers to ingest instantly. |
| **04** | **Citation Monitoring** | Use share-of-voice tracking tools to monitor how often the company's URL appears as an active link within LLM summaries (Google Gemini, ChatGPT, Perplexity). |

---

## 2026 Value for AI & GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)
* **Direct Fact Injection:** By using clear tables, bulleted summaries, and structured definitions, enterprise sites become the default "fact repositories" that AI engines trust and cite when answering complex, multi-variable queries.

---

## Strategic Limitations & Rules
* **The SGE Traffic Drop:** Organizations must brace for an inevitable 20-40% reduction in raw top-of-funnel informational blog traffic, shifting their KPIs toward high-intent branded search and product-led conversions.
* **Zero-Click Containment:** Content layout must place a human-centric call to action (CTA) directly underneath the BLUF summary to entice users to click through for deeper, proprietary files or interactive tools.
